title: "Human-Centered AI for Cybersecurity, Digital Forensics, and Digital Trust"
shortTitle: "HAI for Digital Security"
weight: 3
abstract: >
    The rapid integration of Artificial Intelligence into cybersecurity and digital ecosystems has transformed the way cyber threats are detected, investigated, and mitigated. However, alongside technological advancements, there is an increasing need to ensure that AI-driven security solutions remain transparent, trustworthy, ethical, and centered around human values. This special session aims to bring together researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and practitioners to discuss innovative methodologies, emerging technologies, practical applications, and future challenges in developing human-centered AI solutions that enhance cybersecurity, digital forensic investigations, privacy protection, and digital trust.
