## How to Register

1. **Create an AiScholar account first.** Visit the [AiScholar platform](https://www.aischolar.com/conference/ihci2026/registration) and sign up for an account.
2. **Log in and register for the conference.** Once your account is set up, simply log in and complete your registration for IHCI 2026. You can log back in at any time to view or manage your registration.
3. **Registering for others (optional).** The platform supports registering multiple tickets under one account. For example, a student can conveniently register tickets on behalf of their teacher(s) in a single session.

## Important Information

- **Early Bird Deadline**: 30 September 2026
- **On-Site/Late Registration Deadline**: 26 October 2026
- Please note that, in order for a paper to be included in the proceedings and presented at the conference, at least one author is required to register for a **Full Paper** by the camera-ready deadline.

## Registration Fees

**IHCI Society Membership Discount:** Active IHCI Society members are eligible for a 10% registration discount. Proof of active membership is required. For more information about IHCI Society membership, please visit [https://www.ihci-society.org/members/guide](https://www.ihci-society.org/members/guide).
