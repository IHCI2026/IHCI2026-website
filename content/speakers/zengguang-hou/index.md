  Zeng-Guang Hou is a Professor at the Institute of Automation, Chinese Academy of Sciences (CAS). He is a VP of Chinese Association of Automation (CAA). Dr. Hou is a CAA/ IEEE Fellow. His research area includes medical robotics and computational intelligence for the rehabilitation of motor and cognitive impairments. He was a recipient the National Natural Science Award of China in 2017, the Outstanding Achievement Award of Asia Pacific Neural Network Society (APNNS) in 2017, the Dennis Gabor Award of International Neural Network Society (INNS) in 2023 and the Gold Medal of Geneva International Exhibition of Inventions.
