title: Venue
---

IHCI 2026 will be held at the **University of Nottingham Ningbo China (UNNC)**, located in the vibrant coastal city of Ningbo, Zhejiang Province, China.

On the final morning of the conference (Saturday 31 October), we also offer an optional [half-day tour of Ningbo](/venue/tour/) with three itineraries to choose from: a historic city walk, lakeside scenery, or the city's history and heritage.
